# DeBrief — The Coupling (music reference standard)

The coupling is a STANDARD, not a soundtrack: reference recordings the shipped
score is written against. They are commercial masters and are NOT licensed for
use in the game (sync + master licensing is impractical at our scale). The
shipped score will be original work composed to hit this target, built on the
adaptive stem architecture stolen from the references themselves: stealth /
suspicion / combat / conversation states, after Chaos Theory and Deus Ex.

## The seats

1. THE DEBRIEF ROOM / GREYPORT AT NIGHT — Bohren & der Club of Gore.
   Playlist cut: "Prowler" (Sunset Mission). Reference album: Midnight Radio.
2. THE CITY THEME — Vangelis, "Blade Runner Blues".
3. INFILTRATION TENSION — Amon Tobin, "Ruthless" + "Ruthless (Reprise)"
   (Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory). Both cuts sit in the seat: the reprise is
   the stealth-state variant of the same material.
4. THE SERVICE'S OWN SOUND — Brandon & van den Bos, "Home Base (UNATCO)"
   (Conspiravision: Deus Ex Remixed).
5. THE SIEGE PULSE — John Carpenter, "Assault on Precinct 13" (main title).
6. THE REGIME'S RADIO — Nitzer Ebb, "Join in the Chant" (1987, period-exact).
7. MECHA WEATHER — Mica Levi, "Lipstick to Void" (Under the Skin).
8. EXILE MELANCHOLY — Tangerine Dream, "Love on a Real Train"
   (Risky Business soundtrack cut).

Deliberate absence: no synthwave. Greyport is sodium and wet concrete, not neon.
