# DeBrief — Slice 1: ONE BLOCK OF GREYPORT (milestone brief)

Chase's ruling, 2026-10-29: the shortcut is taken. DeBrief's first slice starts
immediately after C35 M7 lands, on the M7 operator; the Rebuild (M8) then lands
INSIDE the slice and upgrades him in place through the pose-graph seam.

## Home
A new host crate in the C35 workspace (working name `debrief`), consuming the
engine as the windowed host does. Scripted/headless proofs as per house pattern;
PS1.5 presentation is the slice's default mode. Classic-path regressions stay
green byte-for-byte as ever.

## In scope
1. **The block.** One Greyport street block, procedural, zero image assets:
   warehouse row (brick/stone pixel-surface language per golden #9/#11), opposing
   tenement fronts, wet street with sodium lamp pools, the dockyard crane and
   fog backdrop, a three-bars banner, sagging wires, rooftop line. Checkpoint-
   lite furniture (barriers, kiosk) as set dressing. Rooftop/wire anchor points
   recorded in data as FUTURE grapple anchors (no traversal yet).
2. **The operator, in situ.** M7's character + movement model + over-shoulder
   camera, on the street: walk / run / crouch the block end to end.
3. **One patrol.** The Regime soldier as a second procedural character built on
   the operator's part system, re-proportioned to his golden sheet (greatcoat
   split skirt, helmet + blank mask, rifle carried low, three-bars armband on
   the LEFT arm), walking a loop route on the block.
4. **The light read.** The unified-light coupling in embryo: the same practical
   light field that renders the scene answers, as data, whether the patrol can
   see the operator (lit / unlit at his position). Displayed in the ledger, not
   yet an AI. (Full stimulus/response perception is a later milestone.)
5. **Proofs.** Scripted drive: the operator crosses the block in the open, then
   crouched through shadow, with the visibility read logged per segment. Frame
   captures including one composed to echo golden #11 (the patrol under the
   lamp). Cost ledger, determinism, full regression suite.

## Out of scope (named, not forgotten)
Grapple/traversal (next milestone), the operator standard pass (the Rebuild,
landing inside this slice after), perception AI beyond the light read, combat,
the mecha, interiors (the debrief room is its own milestone), the visor
sense-view (its own milestone; the light read is its substrate).

## The bar
Frames from the drive must sit beside golden #9 and #11 without apology on
grade, light, palette, and material language. Character fidelity is knowingly
pre-standard-pass (Chase accepted this trade explicitly) — everything else
meets the standard now.
