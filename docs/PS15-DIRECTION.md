# C35 direction — the PS1.5 target and DeBrief (Chase's directive, 2026-10-29)

**Visual target (Chase's classification): "PlayStation 1.5 — better than PS1, not quite PS2."**
Reference image: `concept-art/operator-reference.jpg` (his upload) — and the
full-body model sheet `concept-art/operator-model-sheet.jpg` (his upload
2026-10-29): the operator standing complete — digital camo, red visor, NSA patch, USP at
ready, radio pack, thigh holster, knee pads, boots. The model sheet governs proportions and
everything below the chest; the bust governs the face and the banner pose. — a chunky
low-poly special-operations soldier at night: blocky readable silhouette, flat-shaded facets,
low-resolution palette-graded surfaces, deep blue night grade with sodium practicals, red visor
glow as the accent, guard towers behind. The man in the image is the main character.

What PS1.5 means as engine doctrine (the reading the crews work to):
- Low-poly geometry with big, readable silhouettes; flat facets and low-res procedural
  textures (the zero-image-asset law stands — surfaces are generated, not loaded).
- Rendered at low internal resolution and presented clean: NO affine texture warp, NO vertex
  wobble (that is the "better than PS1" half), with the temporal reconstruction line (M3/M4)
  serving the clean upscale instead of photoreal detail.
- Fog, dither, limited per-material palettes, baked + vertex-level lighting weight; the
  reality layer (real clock/latitude sun and seasons) still drives the world's light — a
  DeBrief night op at the player's real local night is the same law, new clothes.
- Characters are first-class engine citizens now: low-poly skeletal figures in the
  ~1–3k-tri class, the operator as the reference build.

**The first game: DeBrief** — repo: `nightwarelabz/DeBrief` (public, created by Chase 2026-10-29; LICENSE only at first check, AGPL-3.0 by size). A series. You play as an NSA special agent; the operator in the
reference image is the main character. The name is also the frame: every entry opens and
closes as a debriefing. **First entry: "DeBrief — A city of spies"** (the banner's subtitle,
Chase 2026-10-29; banner art kept at `debrief-banner.jpg` — stone DeBrief
wordmark, the operator full-length at a night dockyard of watchtowers, "made with the C35
engine" line with the rainbow C35 mark, RATING N/A box). (Skelli's ruling when asked for better ideas: DeBrief stands — no
alternative offered beat it.)

Sequencing (unchanged gates): the engine is still not "ready" by Chase's standing ruling —
the playable application waits for his call. What changes is the target the engine depth is
aimed at: M5 (fold occlusion + motion reconstruction) finishes as briefed — both feed this
look directly (baked occlusion is how low-poly terrain gets depth; reconstruction is how the
low-res path stays clean in motion) — and **M6 is the PS1.5 pivot**: the low-res render path
with clean presentation, the palette/texture regime, and the operator character pipeline
(model construction from the reference, rig, idle/weapon poses) as engine capability, proven
in the island host before any game exists.## THE ERA BINDS THE PIXELS, NEVER THE LOGIC (Chase, 2026-10-29)

"just because it is ps1.5 style graphics, doesnt mean our logic has to be bent on the
underworked games of the 2000s etc. we will make the best game of the decade with
DeBrief. all while using classic golden era graphics." The retro discipline applies to
render presentation ONLY. Simulation, AI, systems, and game logic are modern and
uncompromised — no period-limited mechanics imported for authenticity.

## THE ADVERTISED STANDARD (Chase, 2026-10-29)

C35 is ADVERTISED as matching the concept-art style — the public briefing, the banner,
the golden art. That advertisement is a commitment, not an aspiration: engine output that
does not read as that style is a broken promise, whatever its frame times. A concept-art
MP4 from Chase is forthcoming and joins the golden canon on arrival (motion and camera
standards included).

## THE BEAUTY LAW (Chase, 2026-10-29)

"we are not aiming for globby, minecraft shit. we are aiming for our own unique beauty" —
and "we do not stop until the graphics are at the standard." PS1.5 means low triangle
counts with HIGH surface craft: real material read, painted per-pixel surface detail,
clean deliberate silhouettes. Boxy part-built globs, flat undifferentiated facets, and
blobby proportions are the named failure mode. The golden-standard art IS the definition
of the beauty; graphics passes iterate until the renders read at that standard.
